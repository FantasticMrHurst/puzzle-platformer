VIDEO GAME ASSET REFERENCES
Source: uploaded screen recording, approximately 38.8 seconds. Red character sequence: first ~23.6 seconds.
All timestamps are approximate recording times, not YouTube times.
Screenshots: 1536 x 864 cropped gameplay views; resized from the screen recording (which itself contains upscaled pixel art).
Character crops and transparent cutouts: aligned 460 x 494 PNGs; same fixed crop origin for all frames. Sampled at 5 frames/second; these are reference poses, not guaranteed complete animation cycles.
Transparent cutouts: automatic background subtraction, hole filling, and removal of small isolated differences. Some edges, small particles, and shadows may be missing or retained; refine before production. Crate is included in carrying frames.
Environment: reconstructed stationary scene using temporal median of the disappearance frames (22.2–23.6 seconds). The title banner obscures the upper left throughout; the unobstructed scene crops below it instead of inventing hidden art. Individual environment crops are reference regions, not separated parallax layers or seamless tiles.
Motion names are visual interpretations. Original animation art remains the reference; design distinct original assets for your own game unless you have appropriate rights.
