# Gardener Growth Lab

Open `?level=gardener-lab` to test whether growing a plant platform gives the Gardener a useful team role.

1. Select the Gardener (`1`) and approach the seed bed until it shows `PRESS F · GROW`.
2. Press `F` to grow the living platform.
3. With each character, jump onto the plant, land, then jump again to reach the upper ledge. Hold jump through each ascent, especially with the Pet.
4. Bring the Gardener, Brute, and Pet into the shared exit.

Growth persists when you switch characters. Press `F` again near the seed bed as the Gardener to wilt the plant. Press `R` to reset the room and return the plant to its dormant state.

During playtesting, look for whether the seed bed clearly indicates where growth will appear, whether the Pet's two jumps are forgiving, and whether wilting creates useful puzzle choices. Growth timing and ways to combine plants with other abilities are open design questions.
