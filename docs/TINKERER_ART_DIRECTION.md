# Tinkerer Art Direction

## Goal

Design a feminine, humanoid robot Tinkerer whose old, weathered body has become a home for vines and flowers. Match the game's existing detailed pixel-art character sprites, while simplifying the supplied illustrations into a clear silhouette that reads at gameplay scale.

The robot references guide the concept; do not copy either design literally. Use the game sprites for pixel scale, color grouping, outline weight, and detail density.

## Tinkerer

- **Silhouette:** A nimble, medium-build humanoid robot. Use a gently tapered torso, softly rounded shoulder and hip armor, and long practical limbs. Keep the feminine read in the overall shape and posture rather than adding human clothing or exaggerated anatomy.
- **Head and face:** A compact rounded helmet or visor with two bright cyan eyes. Keep the face simple and readable in a small sprite. One small antenna or broken fin can make the head silhouette recognizable from the side.
- **Age and material:** Cool blue-gray and pale teal metal, with dark seams, chipped edges, oxidized patches, and restrained orange-brown rust around joints and plate edges.
- **Plant growth:** A few purposeful vines cross the shoulder, torso, or one leg. Use a small cluster of leaves and one flower near the head as the main accent. Avoid covering the eyes, hands, or major joints.
- **Mechanic identity:** A compact hip tool pouch and one readable tool shape, such as a short wrench, integrated into the silhouette. Keep these details large enough to survive pixel reduction.
- **Pose and expression:** Gentle, curious, and capable. A slightly uneven stance or relaxed hand can suggest age without making movement look impaired.

### Palette

Use a limited palette with clear light and dark pixel clusters:

- Cool blue-gray and pale teal for the outer shell.
- Dark blue-green for joints, recesses, and outlines.
- Muted orange-brown for rust.
- Leaf green for vines, with a small warm cream or coral flower accent.
- Bright cyan for the eyes, used sparingly as the strongest glow.

Keep shading crisp and pixel-clustered. Avoid smooth gradients, thin painterly scratches, and tiny high-frequency details that disappear at the game's sprite scale.

## Companion reference

Treat the supplied many-legged flower robot as a separate character design. Carry over its compact, low silhouette, lily-pad and flower shapes, aqua glass-like section, and warm yellow metal accents. Simplify its legs and plant details into bold pixel clusters. Keep it visibly smaller than the Tinkerer and distinct from the humanoid silhouette.

Do not include the companion in the Tinkerer turnaround sheet; create its own sheet after its proportions and animation needs are set.

## Tinkerer character sheet

Use one consistent full-body scale and neutral standing pose for a clean turnaround:

1. Front view.
2. Three-quarter front view.
3. Side view, facing right.
4. Back view.
5. Three-quarter back view.

Keep the head, panel shapes, vine paths, rust marks, tool pouch, and flower placement consistent across views. Add a small enlarged head/detail view only if needed to clarify the visor, eye pixels, antenna, and flower. Leave animation poses for a later sheet so this one serves as a reliable modeling and sprite reference.

## Gameplay readability checks

- The Tinkerer remains identifiable from its head shape and blue-green shell before the player notices small details.
- Rust, vines, and flowers are visible but do not obscure the robot's joints or limb poses.
- The feminine silhouette stays readable in front, side, and three-quarter views without relying on surface details.
- The companion's flower-and-lily-pad silhouette cannot be mistaken for the Tinkerer at a glance.
